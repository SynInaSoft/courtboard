CourtBoard builds fair doubles and singles draws for club nights — badminton, tennis, padel, beach tennis, pickleball and table tennis. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome:

syninasoft@gmail.com — please include your phone model and app language.

Frequently asked

I bought Pro on another phone.
Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I move my rosters to a new phone?
Settings › Backup › Export backup file, then Import backup file on the new phone. Your data is stored only on your device, so there is no cloud sync.

Players can’t open the QR.
The QR opens a web page in the phone’s browser, so the scanning phone needs internet. The QR changes every round; scan the one on screen now.

How is the draw kept fair?
Game counts never differ by more than one, nobody sits out twice in a row (whenever there are at least as many playing as sitting), and repeat partners and opponents are avoided.

What do I get for free?
Up to 2 courts and 16 players with every format, standings, the big screen and QR. A board already in progress is never blocked.
