CourtBoard Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 · 한국어

Summary

CourtBoard has no accounts. Player names, rosters, draws and scores are stored on your device. Only if you use Live sharing are the current draw, names and scores of that session uploaded to our server, and they are deleted automatically one day after the last update.

Data stored on your device

Names you enter, optional skill levels and gender (used only to build fair teams), draws, scores and settings. You can export or delete them at any time (Settings › Backup, or delete the app).

QR share

When you show the QR, the current draw and standings (names and scores) are encoded inside the link itself, and the viewer page (syninasoft.github.io) reads them in the visitor’s browser.

Live sharing

Live sharing is on by default and can be turned off on the QR screen. While it is on and you have opened the QR or wall screen for a session, the app uploads that session’s title, player names, current matches, queue and standings to our server (Supabase, a hosting provider), so that the viewer page can update by itself. Nothing else is uploaded — no account, contact details or location. The data is reachable only with the 8-character code in the QR link, is deleted when you turn Live sharing off or delete the session, and is otherwise deleted automatically one day after the last update. Anyone who has the link can see what it contains, so share it only with your group.

Usage analytics and crash reports

To improve the app we collect anonymous usage events (for example, “a round was started”, number of courts) with PostHog, and crash reports with Sentry. These never include player names, scores or other content you enter, and are not linked to your identity.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is processed by Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We receive only an anonymous purchase status, never your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
